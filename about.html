About TS Vault

TS Vault is a digital security and solutions brand focused on helping individuals and businesses build a stronger presence in the digital world.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide practical digital solutions that help businesses and individuals navigate the modern digital environment with greater confidence, security and professionalism.

Our Vision

TS Vault aims to grow into a trusted digital solutions brand, creating accessible technology services while continuing to learn, innovate and adapt to an evolving digital landscape.

What We Do

TS Vault brings together digital security, web design and development, and digital marketing under one platform. Each service is designed with the goal of helping clients establish, improve and protect their digital presence.

Behind TS Vault

Thato Seilamashi

TS Vault was founded by Thato Seilamashi with a vision of building a digital solutions brand from the ground up. The journey is centred around learning, creativity, technology and creating solutions that can make a real difference for clients.
